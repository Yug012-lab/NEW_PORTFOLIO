Behind the Code

I'm Yug Verma — a self-taught Full-Stack Developer from Delhi finishing my BCA at IGNOU. I didn't wait for a classroom. I broke things, rebuilt them, shipped until they worked.

My stack is the MERN ecosystem. I've built five real products — an AI chatbot, real-time chat, job tracker, invoice system, and a food management platform. Every project is a chapter. Every bug is a lesson.

At Seoczar, I worked at the crossroads of content and technology — writing for four live client products while collaborating with dev and UX teams. That cross-functional exposure made me a sharper, more empathetic developer.

Great software is invisible to the user. It just works — fast, reliable, beautiful. That philosophy lives in every line I write.
